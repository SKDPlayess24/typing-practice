    Free English typing test and practice

    Touch Type Trainer is a free online typing practice tool for English. Choose a paragraph level and a test length from 1 to 10 minutes, then type while a live words-per-minute (WPM) meter tracks your speed. When the timer ends you get your typing speed, accuracy and the number of backspaces you used. No sign-up is needed.

    How to use it

    1. Pick a level: Easy, Medium or Hard. Each level has 8 paragraphs, or choose Random to keep getting new ones.
    2. Pick a test length from 1 to 10 minutes.
    3. Click the text and start typing. The timer begins with your first key.
    4. Read your result: net WPM, accuracy, backspaces used, mistakes and a speed graph.
    5. Add your own paragraph to practise the words you use at work or in study.

    How speed and accuracy are measured

    Net WPM counts only correctly typed characters, with every five characters treated as one word, divided by the minutes you typed. Gross WPM counts every keystroke. Accuracy is the share of keystrokes that were correct when you pressed them, so fixing a mistake with backspace still counts as a mistake.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a good typing speed?
    Around 40 WPM is a comfortable everyday speed, 60 WPM or more suits most office jobs, and 80 WPM or more is very fast. Keep accuracy above 95% as you build speed.
    Does the typing test cost anything?
    No. It is free to use, with no account or download.
    Are my scores saved?
    Your best score for each test length is saved in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.
    Can I practise my own text?
    Yes. Use "Add your own paragraph" to paste any English text and practise it.
